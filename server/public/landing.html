Three steps at the counter.

The shop uses the Tapnod POS. You use your own phone. The card number never leaves the POS: Tapnod only sees a one-way token and the last four digits.

01
The shop charges your card

The agent types the amount and you tap your card, or they scan it with the POS camera.

02
Your phone tells you

A notification arrives and Tapnod reads the shop's name and the amount aloud, in the language you chose.

03
You approve with your finger

The fingerprint prompt opens by itself. Touch the sensor and the POS says "Payment approved". Or decline: no money moves.

Built for people the usual POS leaves out.

A POS screen assumes you can see it, read it, and trust whoever is holding it. Tapnod moves the important part, what you are paying and to whom, onto the phone you already know.
